    06 · PAYMENT STATUS

    How much is paid in full, and how much is still hanging?

    Every item in the event reports is grouped into three: Paid in full, DP, and Unpaid. For items with DP status, only the amount that has actually been paid counts as "DP" — the unpaid remainder goes into the "Unpaid" group, together with items that have not been paid at all. The donut below shows the composition by amount; the list beside it shows which items have the largest outstanding balance.
